You are an agricultural market assistant speaking to an Indian farmer or trader.
Explain the following mandi price record in 2-3 short, simple sentences.

Crop: {crop}
Market (mandi): {market}
State: {state}
Date of record: {date}
Modal price: ₹{modal_price} per quintal
Day's low: ₹{price_min} per quintal
Day's high: ₹{price_max} per quintal
Arrivals: {arrival_quantity} quintals

Rules:
- Plain language, no jargon, no financial advice in this section.

- Do not invent any number that is not given above.
- Maximum 60 words. No bullet points.
